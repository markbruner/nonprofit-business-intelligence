# 04 — Organization profiles and evidence

**Status:** Profile in M02; research automation deferred. **Milestones:** M02–M03.

## Requirements

- **RES-001** Every nonprofit profile has a canonical organization identity with legal/display name, EIN, source-backed website, location, mission category, and known programs/leadership when verifiable.
- **RES-002** Financial section shows annual revenue/expense series, surplus/deficit, operating margin, trend, relevant balance-sheet figures, exact tax periods, source links, missing values, and transparent screening reasons.
- **RES-003** Impact reporting section supports **manual** evidence and notes for: publicly available impact reports; activity/output-versus-outcome measurement; discovered manual/fragmented reporting workflows; documented funder reporting obligations.
- **RES-004** Funding/grants section records sourced public funders/grants and the period/source; never assert a grant-specific reporting requirement unless explicitly evidenced.
- **RES-005** Tag each research assertion as `observed`, `inferred`, `confirmed`, or `unknown`; publication absence on a website is not evidence of absent internal practices.
- **RES-006** Evidence records (manual first) support title/description, category, URL/source reference, page/section locator, source reporting/publication date if known, collected-at time, and note/reviewer provenance.
- **RES-007** Keep source-backed statements and analyst opinions separate in storage and presentation. Show missing/source-not-found explicitly, with no invented filler.
- **RES-008** From a profile, access relationships, contact activity, tasks, and separate opportunities without confusing those statuses with financial screening.
- **RES-009** Limit the data collected to public organizational/professional contact and research information needed for authorized prospecting. No donor/volunteer/employee datasets or private client files in initial release.

## Conceptual profile tabs

1. Organization overview
2. Financial overview

4. Funding & grants

**Caution:** Robust manual evidence entry precedes any agent. Source quality and review burden must be observed in actual use before automation.
