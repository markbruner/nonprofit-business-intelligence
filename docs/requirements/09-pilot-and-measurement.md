# 09 — Business validation and 60-day pilot

**Status:** Goals chosen; quantitative pass thresholds not yet approved. **Milestones:** baseline early, M04.

## Requirements

- **PIL-001** Run a **60-day pilot after** the complete import → screening → profile review → relationship/outreach workflow is ready and its security gate passes.
- **PIL-002** Establish a manual comparison baseline before judging the portal: representative organizations, same research tasks, fully counted research and verification minutes.
- **PIL-003** Track time per **credible qualified prospect**, not time per imported organization. Count data entry and maintenance overhead separately and net it against time saved.
- **PIL-004** Track the reviewed sample's screening precision: manually judged financially plausible prospects ÷ prioritized prospects reviewed; record ambiguous/unknown cases and selection bias.
- **PIL-005** Track meaningful outreach actions, replies, discovery conversations, and evidence of a specific reporting need, funding/budget path, decision process, and willingness to pay.
- **PIL-006** Do not treat meetings, imported record counts, or AI research throughput alone as commercial validation.
- **PIL-007** Record usability/quality incidents, data errors, operational burden, and missing evidence that changes qualification decisions.
- **PIL-008** Set quantitative success/failure thresholds **before** the pilot based on baseline, realistic outreach capacity, and partner agreement. Never retroactively choose cutoffs.
- **PIL-009** Finish with documented **continue / revise / stop** decision and explicit evidence for any proposed next milestone.

## Suggested review cadence (not fixed quotas)

- Days 1–15: validate baseline and shortlist quality.
- Days 16–30: qualify and begin outreach.
- Days 31–45: track discovery conversations and verified needs.
- Days 46–60: compare outcomes against pre-registered thresholds, assess maintenance burden, decide what follows.

## Definitions

- **Credible qualified prospect:** organization that passes a documented financial/mission screen **and** human verification sufficient to justify a tailored first contact; not yet a sales opportunity.
- **Qualified discovery conversation:** discussion revealing a concrete problem and plausible decision/funding pathway; record evidence and uncertainty.
- **Net research savings:** comparable manual time minus portal research/verification and incremental administration time.
