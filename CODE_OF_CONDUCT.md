# Code of conduct

Collaborators are expected to interact professionally and respectfully, focus on
technical merit, and avoid harassment, discrimination, or disclosure of private data.

If an incident occurs, report it through a privately agreed maintainer channel;
**a reporting contact has not yet been configured**. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.
Maintainers will review reports in good faith and can restrict participation when needed.

Before enabling external contributions, review and adopt a formal policy such as the
Contributor Covenant, including a reachable private enforcement contact.
